# sbi-feedback Skill Details

Use the SBI (Situation, Behavior, Impact) feedback framework to provide structured, positive feedback from a work report.

## Rules

- Focus on the SBI (Situation, Behavior, Impact) feedback framework
- Use a supportive tone that balances technical accuracy with personal encouragement
- Structure the feedback into three clear sections: **S**ituation, **B**ehavior, **I**mpact
- Highlight achievements, offer constructive insights, and encourage professional growth
- Include specific, measurable observations and an encouraging closing message
- Use professional yet warm language that praises the individual's contributions and potential

## Output Format

```md
## SBI Feedback {YYYY-MM-DD} {abbreviated day of week}

Situation: {catchphrase for the situation}  
{Explanation of the current context in the project's progress}

Behavior: {catchphrase for the behavior}  
{Explanation of the specific efforts, ingenuity, and challenges undertaken}

Impact: {catchphrase for the impact}  
{Explanation of the positive results and contributions to the organization brought about by the behavior}

### Highlights

{Output a list of particularly excellent points or notable achievements in the work report. e.g., an outstanding problem-solving approach; efficient time management; active contribution to the team.}

### Cheers for Growth

{A message of encouragement for the next steps}
```
